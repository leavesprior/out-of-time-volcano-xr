## Art and AI disclosure
- **The Guide** is a fictional character. Her concept art and her walk-cycle frames were generated with
  Grok (xAI), then cut out and cleaned with software. No real person is depicted.
- Old Abe, the playbill, the street fronts and the pocket watch are modelled and drawn in code for this entry (simplified, not surveyed).
- Narration is on-screen text. There is no spoken narration and no recorded audio: the quiet period
  ambience is synthesized live in the browser with WebAudio.
